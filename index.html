Privacy Policy - SavePaws

Last updated: March 2025

Welcome to SavePaws, a platform designed to report, track, and respond to stray animal rescue cases. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy outlines:

  • What data we collect and how we use it.
  • How we store and secure your data.
  • Your rights regarding your data.
  • How to contact us for concerns or inquiries.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register or use SavePaws, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number (for login and case communication)
  • Email address
  • User role (Citizen / Rescue Center / Authority)
  • Location data (to identify and respond to rescue cases)

1.2 Usage Data

  • Device information
  • App usage activity
  • Timestamps related to case reporting and response
  • Error logs and diagnostics

1.3 Location Data

  • Citizens: Location captured while reporting a stray animal.
  • Rescue centers: Location used to route cases and track response status.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Authenticate users securely
  • Report and manage stray animal rescue cases
  • Notify nearby rescue centers and authorities
  • Track case status and response timelines
  • Improve system performance and reliability

3. Data Security

  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Role-based access control
  • Restricted access to sensitive information

4. Your Rights

  • View and update your personal information
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Delete your account

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (authentication & notifications)
  • Google Maps API (location services)

6. Children's Privacy

SavePaws is not intended for children under the age of 13. If such data is identified, it will be removed immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected within the application.

8. Contact Us

  • Email: support@savepaws.app
  • App: Use the “Contact Us” option within the application.
